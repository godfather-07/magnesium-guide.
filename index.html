01 · Introduction

Understanding magnesium

Magnesium is an essential mineral found in leafy greens, nuts, seeds, legumes and whole grains. The body uses it in hundreds of enzyme reactions, including those involved in energy metabolism, muscle contraction and nerve signalling.

Most people get some magnesium from food. Whether a supplement adds anything depends on diet, health and medicines, which is why it is a conversation for a healthcare professional rather than a label.

Elemental magnesium
27.5%

A label may show a large blend weight, but only part of it is magnesium. One product in this guide lists 1,600 mg of magnesium salts per serving, which supplies 440 mg of elemental magnesium: 27.5%. Compare products on the elemental figure.

Common forms, without the hype

Glycinate / bisglycinateMagnesium bound to the amino acid glycine. Often chosen for gentler digestive tolerance. Pure bisglycinate is only about 14% magnesium by weight, so capsules can carry modest elemental amounts.
CitrateMagnesium bound to citric acid. Well studied for absorption. At higher doses it can loosen stools in some people.
OxideHigh magnesium content by weight (about 60%), but absorbed less well in studies. Common in lower-cost products.
L-threonateA newer form studied for effects on brain magnesium, mostly in animal work; human evidence is still limited. Low elemental share by weight (roughly 7 to 8%).

How to read a label

Serving size

How many tablets or capsules make one serving. Prices and doses are per serving, not per pill.

Elemental magnesium

The actual magnesium per serving. Ignore the larger compound weight in the title.

Other ingredients

Fillers, binders and coatings. Shorter is not always better, but you should know what is there.

Testing and licences

An FSSAI licence is a legal requirement. A lab certificate for the product itself is a different thing.

Forms differ in absorption and tolerance, but no single form is best for everyone.

02 · Method

How I evaluated products

Each product is scored on five parts, weighted by how much they help a reader make a sound purchase. I look at the supplement facts panel first, then the listing, then the brand's own material.

Formulation quality25%Magnesium form or forms, elemental dose, and whether the mix makes sense.

Quality assurance and testing15%Product-level lab certificates, named labs, licences. Facility icons count for less.

05 · Choosing

How to choose a magnesium supplement

01

Start with elemental magnesium

Find the elemental figure per serving. Ignore the bigger compound weight in the product title.

02

Check the form or forms

Look for named forms and amounts. A blend with undisclosed amounts is harder to judge.

03

Think about total intake

Add food, other supplements and fortified products. Label doses are per serving, and some products need two or more tablets.

04

Look for product-level testing

A lab certificate or named lab for this product is stronger evidence than a facility icon.

05

Compare cost per 100 mg elemental

It evens out different pack sizes and doses. Use it alongside dose, not instead of it.

06

Read claims with care

Pack wording, listing images and reviews may promise more than the label supports.

Before you buy

  • Kidney disease: the kidneys clear excess magnesium, so people with kidney problems should speak to a doctor before using any magnesium supplement.
  • Medicines: magnesium can affect how some medicines are absorbed, including certain antibiotics, osteoporosis medicines and thyroid tablets. Ask a pharmacist or doctor about timing.
  • Pregnancy, breastfeeding and under 18: check with a doctor first.
  • Digestive effects: loose stools, cramping and nausea are the most common side effects of supplemental magnesium, and are more likely at higher doses.
  • Supplements are not medicines and do not replace a varied diet or medical care.
Talk to a professional. This guide is information, not medical advice. Anyone with kidney disease, or taking regular medication, should consult a healthcare professional before starting a magnesium supplement.
06 · About

About this guide

This guide compares four magnesium supplements sold in India, using what is printed on their labels and shown in their Amazon India listings. I wrote it because magnesium labels are hard to compare, and the numbers that matter are rarely the biggest ones on the pack.

I have not tested these products in a lab. Every score, calculation and gap in the data is shown so readers can check the working and reach their own view.
